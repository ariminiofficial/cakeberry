Three crafts, one kitchen.

The same standards run through a morning loaf and a five-kilo wedding cake: good ingredients, patient hands, nothing rushed.

Bakery

Milk loaves, pav and rusks baked before the shops open, so the bread is still warm when it reaches you.

  • Milk loaf, brown bread, ladi pav
  • Jeera, cashew and butter cookies
  • Khari, twists and savoury puffs

Cakes

Birthdays, anniversaries and weddings. Each cake is made to order, from a soft sponge you can taste the care in.

  • Belgian chocolate and fresh cream sponges
  • Edible photo prints and themed designs
  • Multi-tier wedding centrepieces

Pastries

Single portions with Dutch cocoa, fruit and mousse, finished each morning and chilled until you arrive.

  • Truffle slices, red velvet, fruit gateaux
  • Lava cups, donuts and cupcakes
  • Gift boxes for family and corporate gifting
